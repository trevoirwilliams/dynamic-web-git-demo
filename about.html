About our pet family

We’re a Jamaican pet store built around care, kindness, and everyday wellness for the animals we love.

Our story

Paws & Paradise began with one simple goal: to make quality pet care simple, affordable, and joyful for families across Jamaica.

What started as a small neighborhood shop has grown into a trusted pet destination where dogs, cats, birds, and small pets can find reliable products and kind guidance.

We believe every pet deserves safe food, stimulating play, cozy rest, and the love of a family that understands their needs.
